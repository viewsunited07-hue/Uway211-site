WHO WE ARE

A trusted subsection of 211, dedicated to connecting you with financial help.

UnitedWay211 is a specialized subsection of the national 211 network. While 211 connects millions of Americans to local health and human services, UnitedWay211 focuses specifically on financial assistance — grants, funding programs, and emergency relief.

If you have worked for many years and are still experiencing difficulty meeting everyday expenses, UnitedWay211 can help you identify local programs and organizations that may be able to provide support with necessities such as housing, utilities, food, transportation, healthcare, and other essential costs.

FINANCIAL ELIGIBILITY & GOOD STANDING

Understanding eligibility before you apply.

Eligibility requirements vary depending on the specific assistance program and organization providing the support. Applicants may be asked to provide information relating to their income, employment, household circumstances, financial situation, and other relevant records. All documents are confidential.

Where a program considers financial or credit history, maintaining a good banking and credit record may demonstrate responsible financial management and help establish that an applicant is in good standing with their financial institution. Banking information will be required to regularly validate applicant.

It is important to understand that assistance is not the same as a loan. Eligible support may be provided as financial assistance or through other forms of aid and may not require repayment. The terms, eligibility requirements, and amount of assistance depend entirely on the specific program and available funding.

Additional Consideration for Health Challenges

Individuals experiencing significant health challenges or long-term medical conditions may have additional needs that can affect their ability to work or meet ordinary living expenses. Conditions such as arthritis and other chronic health difficulties may be relevant when an assistance program evaluates an applicant's circumstances.

Where applicable, applicants may be considered for additional support based on documented financial hardship, medical circumstances, disability, reduced earning capacity, or increased essential expenses. Additional assistance is not guaranteed and is subject to the requirements and funding of the particular program.
